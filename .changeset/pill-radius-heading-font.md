Two theme tokens. `--mizen-shape-pill-radius` shapes every pill: Switch, Progress, Meter, Badge, Funnel, the Menu switch, the tab indicator, and delta chips. It defaults to a full pill; set it to 0 for square corners. Radio buttons, avatars, and dots stay round. `--mizen-font-heading` sets the family of Heading, page and section titles, Markdown headings, and the titles of Card, Dialog, Sheet, Drawer, Popover, Empty, Error State, and Frame. It inherits the body font until a theme sets it.
